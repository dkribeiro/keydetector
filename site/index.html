How it works

The Krumhansl-Schmuckler key-finding algorithm, in real time.

1 · Analyze

A 16,384-point FFT turns each slice of audio into a spectrum.

2 · Chroma

Energy is folded into a 12-note histogram that builds up over time.

3 · Match

The histogram is compared against all 24 major & minor key profiles.

4 · Result

The best match is your key; the bar shows how clear the winner is.
